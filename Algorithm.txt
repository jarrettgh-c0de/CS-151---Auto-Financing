CS151 Assignment #2
Auto Financing
Detailed Algorithm

1. Start the program.

2. Set the fixed costs that will be used in the program:
   - Gas price = $2.75 per gallon
   - One oil change = $50.00
   - Oil changes per year = 4
   - Basic maintenance per year = $400.00
   - Annual parking pass = $306.00

3. Create variables to store the information entered by the user:
   - Car brand
   - Car name/model
   - Car year
   - Car cost
   - Down payment
   - Yearly interest rate
   - Loan term in months
   - Car miles per gallon
   - Miles driven per year

4. Create variables to store the values that will be calculated:
   - Principal
   - Monthly interest rate
   - Monthly loan payment
   - Yearly fuel cost
   - Annual oil change cost
   - Total annual cost
   - Total monthly cost

5. Ask the user to enter the car brand.

6. Read and store the car brand.

7. Ask the user to enter the car name or model.

8. Read and store the car name or model.

9. Ask the user to enter the car year.

10. Read and store the car year.

11. Ask the user to enter the cost of the car.

12. Read and store the car cost.

13. Ask the user to enter the down payment.

14. Read and store the down payment.

15. Ask the user to enter the yearly interest rate as a percentage.

16. Read and store the yearly interest rate.

17. Ask the user to enter the loan term in months.

18. Read and store the loan term.

19. Ask the user to enter the car's miles per gallon.

20. Read and store the miles per gallon.

21. Ask the user to enter the number of miles they expect to drive each year.

22. Read and store the yearly mileage.

23. Find the amount of money being financed by subtracting the down payment
    from the cost of the car.

    Principal = Car Cost - Down Payment

24. Find the monthly interest rate by converting the yearly interest rate
    from a percentage to a decimal and dividing it by 12.

    Monthly Interest Rate = (Yearly Interest Rate / 100) / 12

25. Calculate the monthly loan payment using the loan payment formula.

    Monthly Loan Payment =
    Principal *
    [Monthly Interest Rate * (1 + Monthly Interest Rate)^Loan Term]
    /
    [(1 + Monthly Interest Rate)^Loan Term - 1]

26. Calculate the number of gallons of gas used in one year by dividing
    the yearly mileage by the car's MPG.

27. Calculate the yearly fuel cost by multiplying the gallons of gas used
    by the cost per gallon.

    Yearly Fuel Cost =
    (Yearly Mileage / Car MPG) * 2.75

28. Calculate the yearly oil change cost by multiplying the cost of one
    oil change by four oil changes per year.

    Annual Oil Change Cost = 50.00 * 4

29. Calculate the yearly amount of the loan payments by multiplying
    the monthly loan payment by 12.

    Yearly Loan Payments = Monthly Loan Payment * 12

30. Find the total annual cost by adding the yearly fuel cost, annual
    parking pass, oil change cost, basic maintenance cost, and the total
    of the 12 monthly loan payments.

    Total Annual Cost =
    Yearly Fuel Cost
    + 306.00
    + Annual Oil Change Cost
    + 400.00
    + (Monthly Loan Payment * 12)

31. Calculate the total monthly cost by dividing the total annual cost
    by 12.

    Total Monthly Cost = Total Annual Cost / 12

32. Display a separator line before showing the results.

33. Display the heading "Cost Analysis."

34. Display the full car name using the car year, car brand, and
    car name/model.

35. Display the principal amount formatted to two decimal places.

36. Display the yearly interest rate formatted to two decimal places
    followed by a percent sign.

37. Display the loan term in months.

38. Display the monthly loan payment formatted as currency with
    two decimal places.

39. Display the yearly fuel cost formatted as currency with
    two decimal places.

40. Display the annual maintenance cost formatted as currency with
    two decimal places.

41. Display the annual oil change cost formatted as currency with
    two decimal places.

42. Display the annual parking pass formatted as currency with
    two decimal places.

43. Display the total annual cost formatted as currency with
    two decimal places.

44. Display the total monthly cost formatted as currency with
    two decimal places.

45. End the program.
